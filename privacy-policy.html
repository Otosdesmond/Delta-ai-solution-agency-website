Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what happens to your information when you visit this website (https://deltaai.solutions), contact us through it, or try one of the linked Deltie demo assistants.

Who this policy covers

This website is operated by Delta AI Solutions Agency, an unregistered sole proprietorship operated by Otomiewo Desmond, based in Oghara, Delta State, Nigeria. We are not currently a registered company; this is a sole proprietorship trading under this name.

What this website itself collects

This website does not currently include any contact form, sign-up form, or account system. It does not use analytics, advertising trackers, or any third-party tracking scripts. Visiting this website alone does not send us any information about you.

If a form is added to this site in future, it will only ask for the information needed to respond to your enquiry (for example, a name and contact method), and this policy will be updated to describe it before it goes live.

What happens when you contact us directly

If you message us on WhatsApp or email us at contact.delta.ai.solution@gmail.com, we receive whatever information you choose to share in that message (such as your name, phone number, and the content of your enquiry). We use this only to respond to you and, if you become a client, to deliver our services. We do not sell this information or share it with third parties for their own marketing.

What happens when you try a Deltie demo

The demo links on this site (Dental, Restaurant, Real Estate, and other industry versions) open separate Deltie applications. If you chat with a demo, your messages are processed by the Deltie assistant to generate a reply, and conversation history may be stored (using MongoDB) so the assistant can maintain context within that session. Demo conversations are used to test and improve the product and are not sold or shared with third parties. Please avoid sending sensitive personal information into a demo chat.

Your rights

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA) 2023, you have the right to ask what personal data we hold about you, request that it be corrected, or request that it be deleted. To make any of these requests, email contact.delta.ai.solution@gmail.com or message us on WhatsApp at +234 704 412 9459, and we will respond as soon as we reasonably can.

Data retention

We keep direct enquiry messages (WhatsApp/email) only as long as needed to respond to you or, for clients, for the duration of our working relationship plus a reasonable period afterward for record-keeping. Demo conversation data may be periodically cleared.

Changes to this policy

If how we handle data changes (for example, if we add a contact form, analytics, or payment processing), we will update this page and change the "last updated" date above.

Contact
